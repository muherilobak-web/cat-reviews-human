A one-star review written by a house cat who rates its human the way a hotel guest rates a resort: the human is the staff, the food bowl is the room service, and a four-minute delay is a serious failure of hospitality. The complaint stays flat and deadpan while the cat's standards do the absurd work, and the sting lands on the final image.

## Run

Run `node main.ts`. The program writes the review line to /output/entry.txt without a trailing newline. It is deterministic, uses no network, and has no dependencies.
